BookEasy HK
Brand Visual Guide

Overview

BookEasy HK uses a quiet premium visual system that blends hospitality
softness with SaaS clarity. The customer-facing UI is light, airy, and
calm, while the merchant dashboard introduces a darker operational mode.
The result feels polished, locally aware, bilingual, and mobile-first.


1. Visual Direction

Core feel:
- Calm
- Premium
- Practical
- Editorial
- Mobile-first
- Bilingual
- Locally tailored to Hong Kong service businesses

Visual character:
- Soft ivory backgrounds instead of harsh white-only layouts
- White cards with light borders and restrained shadows
- Teal as the main trust and action color
- Dark charcoal used for contrast, seriousness, and dashboard framing
- Serif headlines paired with modern sans-serif interface text


2. Typography

Primary display font:
- Fraunces, Georgia, serif

Primary body font:
- Plus Jakarta Sans
- Noto Sans TC for Traditional Chinese support
- System sans-serif fallbacks

Brand effect:
- Fraunces gives the product elegance and a more editorial personality
- Plus Jakarta Sans keeps the interface modern, readable, and clean
- Noto Sans TC helps the bilingual UI feel intentional and legible

Typical type sizes used in the UI:
- Hero heading: text-5xl on mobile, text-6xl on large screens
- Section headings: text-4xl
- Dashboard and settings page titles: text-2xl bold
- Body copy: around 14px to 15px
- Small labels and helper text: 12px
- Utility metadata and compact UI labels: 10px to 11px

Typography behavior:
- Hero headlines use light weight
- Tracking is tight for headlines
- Headline line-height is around 1.1
- Body text is neutral, clear, and easy to scan


3. Color Palette

Primary brand colors:
- Primary: #0F766E
- Primary Hover: #0D9488
- Primary Light: #CCFBF1
- Primary Mid: #14B8A6

Neutral colors:
- Ink: #111111
- Ink 2: #3D3D3D
- Muted Text: #6B7280
- Border: #E5E7EB
- Border Subtle: #F3F4F6
- Background: #FAFAF8
- Surface: #FFFFFF

Semantic colors:
- Success: #10B981
- Success Light: #D1FAE5
- Danger: #EF4444
- Warning: #D97706
- Warning Light: #FEF3C7

Palette interpretation:
- Teal communicates trust, freshness, and product action
- Ivory and white keep the interface soft and premium
- Dark ink tones add seriousness and strong readability
- Semantic colors are used sparingly for states and feedback


4. Component Styling

Cards:
- Usually white with a soft border
- Border radius commonly 12px or 16px
- Light shadows only

Buttons:
- Rounded rectangles with a clean, modern feel
- Primary buttons use teal fills with white text
- Secondary buttons often use white backgrounds with border outlines

Borders and surfaces:
- Thin light-gray borders are preferred over heavy fills
- Surfaces feel layered through spacing and subtle elevation

Focus and interaction:
- Focus uses a teal outline
- Hover states are handled with restrained color shifts


5. Layout Style

Landing page:
- Large editorial headline on the left
- Compact product preview or booking card on the right
- Generous whitespace
- Bordered feature grids and clean section rhythm

Booking flow:
- White cards on soft background
- Teal progress states
- Clear step-based structure
- Calm and customer-friendly presentation

Dashboard:
- Dark sidebar
- Light main content area
- Admin-oriented structure without losing the overall brand feel


6. Motion

Animation style:
- Understated
- Functional
- Polished rather than flashy

Defined motion:
- Fade up: 0.5s ease
- Fade in: 0.4s ease

Motion purpose:
- Support clarity
- Add polish
- Keep the experience feeling lightweight


7. Brand Summary

BookEasy HK presents itself as quiet premium booking software for Hong
Kong service businesses. The UI combines editorial elegance with clean
product structure, making it feel trustworthy to customers and reliable
to operators. It sits between hospitality and software rather than
leaning too far into either one.
