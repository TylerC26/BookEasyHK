BookEasy HK brand memory

Brand position:
BookEasy HK should be described as quiet premium booking software for Hong Kong service businesses. The product balances hospitality softness with SaaS reliability, feeling polished, calm, bilingual, mobile-first, and locally aware.

Visual direction:
- Soft ivory backgrounds
- White surfaces and cards
- Restrained shadows
- Thin gray borders
- Teal trust/action accents
- Dark charcoal contrast for dashboard/admin surfaces
- Editorial serif headlines paired with clean sans-serif UI text

Typography:
- Display font: Fraunces, Georgia, serif
- Body font: Plus Jakarta Sans
- Chinese support font: Noto Sans TC
- Hero heading scale: 5xl mobile, 6xl large screens
- Section headings: 4xl display style
- Dashboard/settings titles: 2xl bold
- Body copy: roughly 14px to 15px
- Small labels/helper text: 12px
- Utility metadata: 10px to 11px

Color palette:
- Primary: #0F766E
- Primary hover: #0D9488
- Primary light: #CCFBF1
- Primary mid: #14B8A6
- Ink: #111111
- Ink 2: #3D3D3D
- Muted: #6B7280
- Border: #E5E7EB
- Background: #FAFAF8
- Surface: #FFFFFF
- Success: #10B981
- Danger: #EF4444
- Warning: #D97706

Brand summary:
The UI should feel calm, premium, practical, editorial, and trustworthy. Customer-facing screens should feel soft and approachable; merchant-facing screens should feel more operational and dependable without breaking the overall brand system.
